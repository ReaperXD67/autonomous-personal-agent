# Promotion brief

Prepare a private decision document. This skill grants no email, publication,
account, spending or scheduling authority. Respect current owner holds; an
agent restart, old approval or request for ideas does not release a mailing hold.

Use current product evidence and the owner's constraints before suggesting
channels. Keep verified facts, owner statements, observations and proposals
separate. A reachable homepage does not establish a working signup, multiplayer
session, payout, security claim or community.

Capture:

- Product URL, observation time, target player problem and the first useful action.
- Exact evidence for supported features, screenshots and end-to-end proof gaps.
- Audience language and stated geography; never invent audience demographics.
- Available people, owned channels, production time and cash budget. A zero-spend
  brief excludes ad purchases, paid placements, rewards and new commercial promises.
- A claim ledger: proposed claim, source, verification status and safer wording.
- Current website, creator-contact and platform restrictions. Review current
  Minecraft usage rules for the actual architecture before advertising portable
  currencies, rewards or server monetization; record unresolved fit as a blocker.

State one measurable activation goal and the evidence needed to observe it.
Rank opportunities by audience fit, usefulness, feasibility, attribution and
permission. Mark uncertain scores as judgment rather than measured performance.
Finish with three prioritized decisions, unresolved owner questions, and a
small next experiment. Avoid forecasts of virality or guaranteed creator results.
