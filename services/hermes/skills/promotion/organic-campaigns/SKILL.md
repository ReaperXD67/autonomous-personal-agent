# Organic campaign concepts

Create private concepts and production briefs. This skill grants no posting,
email, account access, spending or scheduling authority. Preserve owner holds
until an explicit owner release; a creative request is not a release to send.

Start from the claim ledger, available real gameplay and production capacity.
Choose an observable moment that a player would want to show a friend: an
unexpected build, a reversible challenge, a cooperative puzzle or an honest
before/after. Novelty must reveal actual value rather than conceal an advert.

For each concept provide a first-frame image, the first spoken/captioned line,
the action that proves it, a three-beat storyboard and one invitation to try.
Include a silent-caption variant and a practical capture list. Use real product
footage for feature proof; label mockups, reenactments and generated imagery.

Draft a compact portfolio with different creative mechanisms, not cosmetic
headline variations. Score fit, surprise, clarity, shareability and production
effort with reasons. Add one low-effort version that can be produced from
existing approved material. Do not require new product features, giveaways,
commission terms or fabricated reactions to make a concept work.

Define an observable success event and a failure signal for each. A compelling
concept is a hypothesis, not evidence that it will become viral. Preserve
creator independence, rights to footage/music and disclosure of relationships.
End with the strongest feasible concept, why it leads, and what must be verified
before the owner can approve publication.
