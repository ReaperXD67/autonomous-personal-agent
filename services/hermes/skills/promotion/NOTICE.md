# Reviewed organic promotion skill pack

Eight original, project-scoped procedures synthesize selected marketing
planning patterns from Corey Haines's MIT-licensed Marketing Skills. The pack
does not vendor or install that full collection, its scripts, integrations or
dependency graph. The upstream license notice is retained in `LICENSE`.

Inspected source: `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` at
`3e729622e3de0c82abdb906190d91f9018045872` (November 11, 2026).

Selected inputs: `marketing-plan`, `community-marketing`, `influencer-marketing`,
`ab-testing`, `analytics` and `free-tools`. Their useful patterns are current-state
intake, member value, source-grounded partner fit, one-variable experiments,
matched measurement windows and useful product-adjacent artifacts. This pack
changes the audience to Minecraft players, binds claims to product evidence,
removes paid placements and new offers, and preserves the control-plane review,
owner holds, contact provenance and exact-message sending boundaries.

Native compatibility was inspected against `NousResearch/hermes-agent`
`v2026.8.3`, commit `3c27eb6234bf91b8ceee9e9071591b31e9b148cb`:
standard name/description frontmatter, categorized `~/.hermes/skills` discovery
and progressive disclosure. No runtime upgrade is implied.

The source manifest pins every packaged file's SHA-256. The networkless
installer copies only the owned `hermes-promotion` category before Hermes starts.
It refuses modified, extra or symlinked owned files rather than overwriting
private agent work. A package checksum proves byte integrity against the
reviewed repository snapshot, not the quality or authority of its instructions.

These skills are knowledge documents, not executable capabilities. They do not
connect accounts, call providers, schedule campaigns, mail creators or publish.
Current owner instructions and the trusted control plane remain authoritative.
