# Community fit

Prepare a private placement assessment and unsent contribution drafts. This
skill grants no posting, messaging, membership, account or spending authority.
Respect owner holds and each destination's current rules; do not infer approval
from a skill installation or service startup.

Use the actual public community rules and a dated source. Record whether
self-promotion is allowed, restricted to a thread, requires moderator permission,
or is unclear. A broad platform policy does not establish a specific server or
subreddit's permission. Unavailable rules mean unknown, not allowed.

Assess the community's topic and language from observed material. Propose a
member benefit that stands alone: an original map-making lesson, reproducible
challenge, useful free asset or candid technical case study. A link dump or a
disguised testimonial is not a contribution.

For each plausible placement record:

- Community, canonical rules URL, date and exact relevant restriction.
- Audience fit evidence and unresolved access or moderation questions.
- The contribution's useful takeaway, relationship disclosure and optional link.
- A draft appropriate to the permitted format and a proposed moderator question
  when permission is required. Both remain unsent.

Exclude repeated cross-post blasts, scraped personal contacts, bought engagement,
sockpuppets, unsolicited DMs and vote manipulation. Do not manufacture accounts
or pretend to be an unaffiliated player. Prefer a few useful contributions and
responses to actual invited questions. Track accepted placement and meaningful
product activation separately from impressions and upvotes.
