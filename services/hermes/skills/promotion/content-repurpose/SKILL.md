# Repurpose a real story

Prepare private content drafts. This skill grants no posting, email, scheduling,
account or spending authority. Preserve the owner's current mailing and release
holds; preparing variants does not release them.

Start with an owned or licensed source artifact and its claim ledger. Record
what is actually visible, its capture date, reuse rights and any missing proof.
Do not turn a storyboard, synthetic scene or creator's unapproved material into
documentary evidence.

Extract a single useful idea, then adapt its presentation to each proposed
channel: a visual short that demonstrates the result, a practical community
post that teaches the method, and a concise owned-site explanation that helps a
new player act. Keep the core claim consistent while changing the opening,
length, context and invitation. Translate English/Polish naturally; preserve
technical meaning and mark translations needing a fluent review.

For every version provide the complete draft, required footage or images,
captions, accessibility text, attribution/disclosure and one relevant action.
Avoid recycled identical cross-posts, invented testimonials, fake scarcity,
unsupported superlatives and engagement bait that obscures product value.

Use campaign-specific link labels from the existing promotion kit when supplied.
Never put names, email addresses or private identifiers in tracking parameters.
Give each variant an artifact ID, review status and measurement hypothesis.
Recommend which versions to produce first from current resources and which to
defer. An asset being finished does not establish permission to distribute it.
